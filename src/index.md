# Welcome to Twin Cape

Redefining Canadian asset management.

## Who We Are

Twin Cape Asset Management is a Canadian investment management firm based in Toronto, Ontario.

We leverage proprietary insights and in-house technology to offer innovative investment services to our clients.

Find out [what we offer](offerings.html).

## What Makes Us Unique

- **Founder-led.** We are an entirely founder-led and operated investment firm, which gives us the flexibility to realize our complete expression of alpha within your portfolio.
- **A global, multi-asset perspective.** We invest without boundaries across asset classes, geographies, and sectors to deliver on our ambitious mandates.
- **Deep research and insight.** Our proprietary investment process has been thoughtfully designed to set us apart. We leverage the latest academic research and technological innovations to improve outcomes for our clients.

## Our Approach

1. **Understanding the needs of our clients.** Our partners expect a premier investment experience, which we are challenged to deliver every day.
2. **Taking a long term view.** We take a long term view on both investments and people. We encourage partners to become familiar with our approach many years before ever writing a check.
3. **Producing delightful investment outcomes.** The caliber of our partners demands that we realize the full force of our financial acumen to deliver the very best results.
