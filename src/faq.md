# Q&A

You questions, our answers.

## Investment Philosophy

1. **What is your investment philosophy?** Twin Cape takes a blended approach to investing with elements borrowed from a number of well-known approaches. Our [investment philosophy](philosophy.html) is unique because we continuously improve our processes over time.

2. **What is your edge?** Our edge comes from our focus on earning satisfactory returns for clients.

## Macro

1. **How does macroeconomic analysis enter into your investing process?** We do not make macro calls. Instead, the macroeconomic environment informs our judgement of the outlook for the securities we assess.

2. **Do current events and geopolitical conflicts affect your investments?** We do not make event-driven calls. We prefer to focus on the longer-term trajectory of our investments and opportunities.

3. **How do your views on broader markets influence your holdings?** We do not make directional calls on broader markets, including popular indices, interest rates, or currencies. We believe that buying securities at low prices will create long-term value, regardless of the direction or velocity of broader markets.

## Portfolio Construction

1. **Do you use leverage?** No. In fact, our High-Conviction Flagship strategies commonly hold liberal cash reserves, which we think of as "negative leverage". Whereas traditional portfolio managers aim to reduce exposure correlation and increase leverage to achieve a target return, we aim to earn a satisfactory return on our committed capital and retain sufficient cash reserves to capitalize on further opportunities.

2. **How do you determine sector allocations?** We do not make sector-level calls as we believe sectors are made of companies, making the proper level of analysis the company-level.

3. **How do you determine geographic allocations?** We exclusively buy U.S.-listed securities.

## Valuation

1. **What sources of data do you use as valuation inputs?** We leverage a variety of data sources, including: **1)** company filings; **2)** news; **3)** institutional data; **4)** anecdotes and commentary; and, **5)** alternative data.

2. **What valuation methodologies do you use?** We use a wide range of valuation methodologies. Generally, our holding-period return depends on the ultimate sale price of the security and any interim cash flows we receive. We triangulate a number of factors to determine what we'd like to pay, given our view of the ultimate sale price and the cash flows — and then we do our best to by at prices far below that level.

## Implementation

1. **How do you decide when to buy / sell a security?** We buy securities when they trade at prices that are far below what we'd be willing to pay. We only sell securities as a source of funding when we discover other, more attractive opportunities.

2. **Do you use quantitative analysis or AI?** We leverage quantitative analysis and AI extensively in our non-investment functions.
