# Q&A

You questions, our answers.

## General Information

1. **What is Twin Cape?** We are a Canadian hedge fund offering a variety of strategies that suit diverse client needs. Our goal is to redefine Canadian asset management.

2. **Who is eligible to invest?** We have no minimums. However, we maintain strict eligibility criteria, evaluated on a case-by-case and product-by-product basis. Twin Cape funds are not suitable for all investors.

3. **Could I lose money investing in Twin Cape?** Yes. Any investment carries the risk of total loss, including one in Twin Cape funds. We make it a priority to understand the needs of every client and make a suitability assessment for every potential investment.

## Investment Philosophy

1. **What is your investment philosophy?** We take a blended approach to investing with elements borrowed from a number of well-known approaches. Our [investment philosophy](philosophy.html) is unique because we continuously improve our processes over time.

2. **What is your edge?** Our edge comes from our focus on earning satisfactory returns for clients.

## Macro

1. **How does macroeconomic analysis enter into your investing process?** We do not make macro calls. Instead, the macroeconomic environment informs our judgement of the outlook for the securities we analyze.

2. **Do current events and geopolitical conflicts affect your investments?** We do not make event-driven calls. We prefer to focus on the longer-term trajectory of our investments and opportunities.

3. **How do your views on broader markets influence your holdings?** We do not make directional calls on broader markets, including popular indices, interest rates, or currencies. We believe that buying securities at low prices will create long-term value, regardless of the direction or velocity of broader markets.

## Portfolio Construction

1. **Do you use leverage?** No. In fact, our High-Conviction Flagship strategies commonly hold liberal cash reserves, which we think of as "negative leverage".

2. **How do you determine sector allocations?** We do not make sector-level calls as we believe sectors are made of companies. As a result, our sector views are formed by our company-level analyses.

3. **How do you determine geographic allocations?** We exclusively buy U.S.-listed securities.

## Valuation

1. **What sources of data do you use as valuation inputs?** We leverage a variety of data sources, including: **1)** company filings; **2)** news; **3)** institutional data; **4)** anecdotes and commentary; and, **5)** alternative data.

2. **What valuation methodologies do you use?** We use a wide range of valuation methodologies. Additionally, we triangulate a number of factors to determine what we'd like to pay, and then we do our best to by at prices far below that level.

## Implementation

1. **How do you decide when to buy / sell a security?** We buy securities when they trade at prices that are far below what we'd be willing to pay. We only sell securities as a source of funding when we discover other, more attractive opportunities.

2. **Do you use quantitative analysis or AI?** We leverage AI extensively in our non-investment functions. While we continue to evaluate the use of AI in our investment operations, we have found no meaningful use cases to-date. However, we make substantial use of quantitative methods throughout the firm.
