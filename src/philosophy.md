# Our Philosophy

*"You can't win if you don't play. So play." -- someone*

Our investment philosophy is the result of an entire career of research, analysis, and experience. We regard our investment philosophy as a living document - one which reflects our learnings over time and updates as we research and develop novel approaches and methods.

As of writing, we would triangulate our philosophy using the following notes:

1. **We make big calls.** We care about the long-term trajectory of our client's investments. As a result, we avoid predicting the next quarter's earnings or analyzing the thoughts and speeches of Presidents. We worry about the big ideas that impact long-term returns.

2. **We care what we pay.** Every investment is a good one at a low enough price. We spend little time figuring the *fair* value of the securities we buy and more time figuring the *unreasonably cheap* price at which we would like to buy - and we do our best to buy at prices far below that threshold.

3. **Following the crowd can be lucrative.** We don't need to be contrarian to make money. We just need to be right. Whether we are with or against the crowd does not matter to us. What matters is that our clients end up better off because of the decisions we make.

4. **Flexibility above all.** In a business defined by uncertainty, we value the ability to change course when the environment demands. We strive to preserve and build on that ability with every decision.

5. **We pick securities.** We buy stocks, bonds, commodities, currencies, and assets. We don't know how to buy "China" (countries) or "Energy" (sectors). Unlike nearly every other manager, we only build our macro, sector, and geographic views into our assessments of individual securities. We are truly *bottoms-up*.

6. **We never compromise on integrity.** We care what managers choose to tell us and what we have to find out on our own. We will ruthlessly scrutinize the integrity of managers, because we have no choice but to rely on them.

7. **We invest in what we know.** We are happy to "miss out" on lucrative investments if we do not know how to price them. The universe is plentiful enough for us to find our fill elsewhere.

8. **When we make calls, we make bold calls.** Investing is binary: you either make money or you don't. When we believe we will lose money, we will pass over the investment and when we believe we will make money, we will lean into it.

9. **We make money.** The plumber is judged by how may toilets (s)he fixes and the mailman is judged by how many letters (s)he delivers. We are judged by how many dollars we produce and we never lose sight of that.

10. **We love what we do.** For many, the investment profession is old, gray, and boring. We think the opposite. We started in this business as children out of genuine interest. We think it's the most dynamic way to create, grow, and preserve a legacy. Nothing excites us more.
