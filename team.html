Eric Ding, CFA
Founder and Principal Portfolio Manager

Eric is the Founder and sole owner/operator of Twin Cape. Eric began his career in Institutional Derivatives Sales and Rates Trading, Cross-Border Mergers & Acquisitions, and Real Estate at top global firms. He has since held stints contributing to North American Industrials and Base Metals and Mining coverage on #1-ranked Equity Research desks. Most recently, Eric supported Investment Committee decision-making across Global Public Equities, Fixed Income, Private Equity, Real Estate, Secondaries, and Venture Capital at one of Canada's fastest-growing asset managers.

Eric holds a BCom/BA dual degree in Business and Computer Science from Queen's University. Eric is also an amateur pianist and an avid ice hockey fan.
